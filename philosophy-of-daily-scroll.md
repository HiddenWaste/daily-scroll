# Why The Daily Scroll?

Algorithmic social media has become the scourge of our mental health, time, productivity, and social cohesion. For myself social media had became the first thing I checked when I woke up, and the last thing I saw before I went to sleep. Some of the apps, such as tik tok, have been engineered in such a way that tries to get you to spend as much time as possible on the app, which has such a powerful and well made algorithm to keep serving you more and more targeted content, again, in an effort to extract as much of your time and attention as possible.

The goal of this project is to leverage a high knowledge and capability with technology to ultimately use technology less. To create a dynamic, programmable personal data feed of which you don't necessarily *need* a digital screen to view. To be printed, or read on an e-ink device. Or at minimum, to eliminate the algorithmically created media feeds in your daily information consumption.
